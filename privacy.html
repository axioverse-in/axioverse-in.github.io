PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Axioverse is engineered on cryptographic minimalism—verifying state without harvesting personal identity.

01 /Zero-Knowledge Core Principle

The Axioverse Spatial Protocol operates without centralized user accounts, behavioral profiling, or third-party ad trackers. Authentication and world ownership rely exclusively on client-side cryptographic keypairs and zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs).

02 /Minimal Data & Node Telemetry

When interacting with Axioverse web interfaces or edge RPC gateways, we process only ephemeral technical metadata required for routing:

  • Ephemeral Session Routing: Transient IP headers are stripped at the edge load balancer and are never written to persistent storage.
  • Local Client Storage: Interface preferences and spatial cache states remain locally in your browser's storage and can be cleared at any time.
  • Voluntary Communications: If you reach out via our Contact portal, submitted details are used solely to respond to your inquiry.

03 /Public Ledger & Spatial State

Transactions, deployed realms, and governance votes broadcast to the Axioverse mainnet are recorded on an immutable public ledger. While wallet addresses are pseudonymous, on-chain state transitions are publicly verifiable by design.

04 /Cryptographic Security

Private keys never leave your local signing environment. All gateway communications enforce TLS 1.3 encryption and deterministic state verification across distributed validator nodes.

05 /Sovereign User Rights

You retain full custody over your cryptographic identity and off-chain communications. For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests regarding direct support messages, contact privacy@axioverse.network.
