TELEXPORT PRIVACY POLICY

TelExport (“the extension”) exports the messages and participants of the Telegram Web chat that you have open into an Excel workbook. This policy explains what the extension handles and where that data goes.

In short: everything is processed and stored on your own device. TelExport does not send data to the developer or to anyone else, makes no network requests, and has no servers, analytics, telemetry, advertising or cloud services.

01 /What the extension accesses

When you use it on web.telegram.org (or webk.telegram.org / webz.telegram.org), the extension reads what Telegram Web has already rendered in your open tab for your logged-in account:

  • message text, message IDs and message times;
  • participant display names and Telegram user IDs shown on the page;
  • the member list, if you open it;
  • only if you turn on “Include media screenshots”: pictures of media that the page has already rendered.

The extension does not read your Telegram login, password, phone number or session credentials, and it does not access any other website.

02 /Where data is stored

  • Extracted messages, participants and optional screenshots are stored in the extension’s local database (IndexedDB) inside your browser profile.
  • Your settings (media on/off, loading patience, optional own Telegram ID) are stored using Chrome’s local extension storage.
  • Exported files (.xlsx, .csv, .txt) are created in your browser and saved to your Downloads folder.

This data never leaves your device through the extension.

03 /What is shared

Nothing. The extension does not sell, share, transfer or transmit user data to anyone. It does not use data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending decisions, or for any purpose other than creating the export you request.

04 /Network activity

The extension itself makes no network requests, and its content security policy blocks connections from its own pages. Telegram Web loads older messages over its own connection when the page scrolls; that is Telegram’s normal operation and is governed by Telegram’s own privacy policy.

05 /Your control

You can delete the saved data for one chat, or all saved data, at any time from the extension popup. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data from your browser. Files you download are yours to manage. You are responsible for handling other people’s names, IDs and messages in line with applicable laws and Telegram’s terms.

06 /Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

07 /Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Any future feature that sends data off your device would be disclosed here before release.
